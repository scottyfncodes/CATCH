# Before a public commercial release: things for legal review

CATCH. is a friends game today. Before it is released commercially (paid, ad
supported, listed in an app store, or marketed), have counsel look at the items
below. Each says what it is, where it lives, and what has already been done.

## Open

### 1. The signature windups (real pitchers' deliveries)

Six windup presets, shown as #34, #16, #35, #32, #51 and #55, were built by
studying the deliveries of well-known real pitchers. In the product they appear
only as a number and a plain description of the motion: no names, nicknames,
likenesses, photographs, team names, colours or logos, and no claim of
endorsement. The real names are deliberately not written anywhere in this
repository.

That is not clearance. A jersey number together with a distinctive,
recognisable motion can still identify a real athlete, and right-of-publicity
law varies by jurisdiction. What to review, and the built-in fallback (ship only
the neutral house delivery, #00), are in [WINDUPS.md](WINDUPS.md#-legal-review-before-any-public-commercial-release).

## Done

### Caps (changed)

The free caps had a single embroidered letter C on the front. A lone C on a
navy, green or red cap sits close to how some professional baseball teams brand
theirs. The caps now carry the game's own wordmark, **CATCH.** (heavy, tight,
the dot in stitch red; in the lettering's colour on a red cap), the same mark as
the home screen. Nothing else about the caps changed.

### The 1500-streak cape (replaced)

The 1500-streak reward was a "Superhero cape": a blue top, a red cape, a belt
and a letter in a diamond on the chest, a combination that echoed a famous
comic-book hero's shield. It is now **The Closer** (baseball slang for the
pitcher who finishes the game): a navy top with a baseball across the chest, a
cream cape with a baseball's red stitching down both edges, and a gold clasp
set with the CATCH. red dot. The emblem, the blue-and-red scheme and the belt
are gone; the streak that earns it and the cape are unchanged. A unit test
keeps "super" and "hero" out of the reward names.

### Nothing else that is known

No team names, league marks, real stadiums or real people's names or likenesses
appear in the game. The other rewards (a propeller beanie, a rubber duck, a
ten-gallon hat, a crown, a Hawaiian shirt, a tuxedo, a royal robe, googly eyes,
a shark, a disco ball, flames) are generic.
